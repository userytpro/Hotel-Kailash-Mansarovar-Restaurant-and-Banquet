What is the listed room price?

How can I confirm a room?

Send your check-in date, check-out date, number of guests and room preference through the enquiry form or call the hotel.

Is Wi-Fi available?

Yes. Complimentary Wi-Fi is included in the supplied amenities information.

Is breakfast available?

Breakfast is listed among the hotel's food and drink facilities. Contact the hotel to confirm serving times and inclusions.

Is airport transportation available?

A free airport shuttle is listed. Please contact the hotel ahead of arrival to confirm the schedule and pickup process.

Can I bring a pet?

Pets are listed as allowed at no charge. Confirm the current pet policy and any conditions directly with the hotel.

Does the hotel have a spa or hot tub?

The supplied listing indicates that there is no spa and no hot tub.

Can I enquire about a banquet event?

Yes. Contact the hotel with your event date, estimated guest count and preferred arrangement to discuss the available options.
